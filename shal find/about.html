ABOUT SHALLY FIND

Making local skills easier to discover.

Shally Find is a directory concept designed to help people discover technicians and local service providers across Nigeria.

Our idea

People should be able to search by service and area, understand what a provider offers, compare indicative prices and contact the provider to discuss the job.

Trust matters

A public listing is not the same as a verified professional. Shally Find should clearly show what has—and has not—been checked before displaying any verification label.

Built step by step

This first version is a front-end demo. Online accounts, shared listings, reviews, moderation and payments require a secure backend before a public launch.
